BimCraft works with fingers as well as with a mouse. The first time you touch the screen, buttons, tabs and list rows grow to finger size.

## The 3D model

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Orbit | Drag one finger |
| Zoom | Pinch two fingers apart or together |
| Pan | Drag two fingers together |
| Pick an element | Tap it |
| Add an element to the pick, or take it out | Press and hold on it for half a second (the same as {{key:Ctrl}}+click) |
| Pick nothing | Tap empty space |

A pinch or a two-finger drag never picks anything, even when a finger lifts over an element.

## Drawings (DWG and DXF)

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Pan | Drag one finger |
| Zoom | Pinch; the point between your fingers stays put |
| Show the whole sheet | Double-tap, or tap {{ui:Fit}} |

In a drawing's {{ui:3D}} view, the model's gestures apply: one finger orbits, two pinch and pan, a tap picks, a long press adds to the pick.

## Walk

Tap {{ui:Walk}}, then tap the floor where you want to stand. A row of arrow buttons appears in the walk bar:

- Hold an arrow to walk forward, back, or step to the side.
- With {{ui:Gravity}} off, the double arrows rise and sink.
- Drag one finger on the model to look round.
- Tap {{ui:Done}} to stop.

## Placing library items

Tap where the item goes. {{ui:Turn 90°}} on the placing bar turns it (the {{key:R}} key on a keyboard), {{ui:Wall snap}} snaps its back to the nearest wall, and {{ui:Stop placing}} ends.

## Measure

Tap the first point, then the second. Points snap to corners and edges near your tap.

:::tip
With a keyboard attached to a tablet, every shortcut works as on a computer. See [Keyboard shortcuts](topic:keyboard).
:::
